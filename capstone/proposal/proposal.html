Spring Quarter 2025 Capstone Project Proposal

For my capstone project, I plan on focusing on exploring artificial intelligence, specifically the rising popularity of LLMs and chatbots. I want to imagine a possible dystopian future involving the consequences of artificial intelligence being driven by capitilistic greed.

Since I like making games, I decided I wanted to make a game that tells a story that comments on the rise in parasocial relationships due to social media, and how the involvement of artificial intelligence can drive this issue to even darker waters.

Currently, my vision is to create a game following a young girl who believes that she is texting with her celebrity crush, whose company implemented an AI chatbot to interact with fans, and to explore how this money-grab from a large corporation can feed into dangerous delusions.

My main concern for this project is that my idea isn't serious enough in terms of a lot of the topics we've discussed, but I feel like storytelling could be an effective way to encourage people to consider the possible ramifications of progress driven by profit. There is much to say about how new artificial intelligence softwares exploit young people with media presence, including the rise of deepfakes. I think that this story could not only touch on capitalism and parasocial relationships, but also how our society fails to protect its people in favor of cutting edge innovation.
