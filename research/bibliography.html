Annotated Bibliography

Jackson, M. “The Hidden Price of Climate Change: Crash Course Climate & Energy #11.” YouTube, Crash Course, 12 Apr. 2023, youtu.be/ML70W4O6F4E?si=E-UdKaPp9VpA1F0C.

In her episode of Crash Course Climate and Energy, Jackson dives deep into the critical need to reduce greenhouse gas emissions to avoid the worst effects of climate change, primarily by transitioning away from fossil fuels. While many countries aim to be carbon-neutral by 2050 and the necessary technology exists, the transition is difficult due to economic, political, and social challenges.

Nicholas, Kimberly. Under the Sky We Make : How to Be Human in a Warming World. G.P. Putnam’s Sons, an imprint of Penguin Random House LLC, 2021.

In her book, Dr. Nicholas explores what it means to find a purpose in a rapidly warming world. She is able to offer a hopeful, but also realistic guide to creating real change,starting in our own lives. She argues that while difficult, preventing the climate apocalypse is possible, yet it requires radical change as a collective.

Pedlar, John. “Tree Movements Promote Carbon Sink.” Nature Climate Change, vol. 14, no. 8, 2024, pp. 785–86, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41558-024-02059-2.

As climate change threatens the ability of forests to act as carbon sinks, assisted migration is emerging as a promising solution. Pedlar covers research tracking which plants are best suited in boosting carbon storage, paving the way for potential Earth-saving innovation.
