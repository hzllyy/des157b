Comparative Analysis

For my first website, I decided to look at the Climate Clock website, which is an organization dedicated to spreading awareness about our climate crisis. This organization first popped on my radar when they installed a climate clock in New York, where it shows a timer counting down the time we have left before our climate crisis is irreversible. The website offers more statistics, including the percentage of our energy that is renewable, the amount of money that will be saved if we act now, and the percentage of Earth's land and water that would be protected. The visualization of these statistics are in bold colors, pushing a sense of urgency to the viewers to act now.

The second example I decided to look at is mobile game called Carbon Warfare, a game in which the player plays as climate change. The goal of the game is to increase global carbon emissions and wipe out humanity, highlighting how destructive unchecked industrialization is. The game's interface is not only interactive and engaging, but also informative, giving players different natural disasters to inflict on cities, as well as providing information on how each disaster would affect each city.
